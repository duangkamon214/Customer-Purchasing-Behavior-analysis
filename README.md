# Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis
## Project Overview
Data analysis project exploring customer purchasing behavior and order patterns to support business decision-making.
## Objectives
- Practice using Python for data analysis
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze products with the highest number of purchases
- Analyze the time and day when customers make purchases
## Dataset
This dataset contains customer purchasing behavior and order information.
- It has a total of 2,019,501 rows and 12 columns.
- The dataset includes information about customers, orders, products, departments, purchase days, purchase times, and reorder behavior.
## Anaiysis
- Top 10 Most Ordered Products
- Number of Purchases by Day of the Week
- Purchase Time Periods
- Repeat Purchase Behavior
  * Department with the Highest Repeat Purchase Rate
  * Top 10 Products with the Highest Repurchase Rates
- Repeat Purchase Frequency
## Key Findings
- Fresh fruits had the highest number of items ordered.
- Most customers bought products on Day 0, followed by Day 1, while Day 4 had the lowest number of purchases.
- The afternoon was the time when the most customers made purchases.
- The Dairy and Eggs department had the highest repeat purchase rate.
- Milk had the highest repeat purchase rate among products.
- The most common time for customers to make a repeat purchase was 30 days.
